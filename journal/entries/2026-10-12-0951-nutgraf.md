# Nutgraf

2026-10-12 09:51. Previous: [2026-10-11-1444-today-browse-and-the-console.md](2026-10-11-1444-today-browse-and-the-console.md)

## What happened

The app has a name: Nutgraf. A nut graf is the paragraph in a news story that says why it matters. It lives at https://sandip.dev/nutgraf/ now.

A second Claude Design handoff came back this morning. It kept every screen from yesterday and changed three things: the name, a logo, and the look. It spelled the name Nurgraf throughout; that was a typo, and I caught it before anything shipped.

The look is Apple's now. SF Pro for everything, New York only for quotes, and the system greys: true black in dark mode, #F2F2F7 in light. Yellow is still the only highlight, and there is no blue anywhere. Newsreader, IBM Plex and Courier Prime are gone, so the app downloads no fonts at all (#73).

The logo is a folded page: a black page with its top-right corner turned down in yellow, over a white n. A turned-down corner is the oldest way to keep your place, which is what Continue and Saved are for. It is the home-screen icon, the tab icon, and the tile on the Mac's top bar.

A tap on a row's title opens the issue now. A tap anywhere else on the row still peeks, and a double tap still opens (#74).

The address moved from /thelibrary to /nutgraf (#75). Every old link answers with a permanent redirect to the same page, filters and search included, so bookmarks and the old home-screen icon still land. The first deploy's hosting check failed two of its 32 checks: it expected one hop where Amplify takes two, and the filters in their original order. The redirects themselves worked. #76 makes the check follow each link to where it ends.

## Where we are now

Nutgraf is on prod at sandip.dev/nutgraf, in the new look, and /thelibrary redirects there. It was tested in mock mode and by following the old links on prod; nobody has used it on a real iPhone yet. The repository, the AWS stacks and the SSM paths keep the old name.

## Decisions

- The name is Nutgraf, `nutgraf` in the wordmark. The Library tab keeps its name. The repository, stacks, SSM paths and storage keys keep "library", so nothing had to be rebuilt and a signed-in session carries over.
- System fonts only, and every background from the handoff's table of exact values.
- The folded page is the icon, drawn from SF Pro's own n so the SVG needs no font.
- A tap on the title opens; the rest of the row peeks.
- /thelibrary redirects to /nutgraf with a 301 instead of staying a second address.
- The rename went out as three pull requests: the look, the title tap, then the address.
- The about, privacy and terms pages say The Library until Google's consent screen is renamed.
- Claude merged #73, #74 and #75 on my word.

## Direction changes

- Name: was The Library, now Nutgraf.
- Address: was sandip.dev/thelibrary, now sandip.dev/nutgraf.
- Type: was Newsreader, IBM Plex and Courier Prime, self-hosted; now SF Pro and New York, from the system.
- Colour: was warm near-black and warm off-white; now true black and Apple's #F2F2F7.
- Icon: was a typewriter L in the yellow box; now the folded page.
- A tap on a row: was a peek anywhere on the row; now the title opens the issue.
