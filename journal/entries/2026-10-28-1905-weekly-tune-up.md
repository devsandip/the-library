## What happened

Library has a Tune-up page. When I keep moving one newsletter's stories to the same shelf, it proposes the rule that would have done it for me, "always Learning" or "always News", with the numbers behind it: how many stories I moved that way against Jev, and what the rule would move that I have not. Nothing changes until I tap Apply, which sets the newsletter's own setting; Not now hides it until I move more. Under the rules it lists my corrections per newsletter, and the promos I pulled back out of Archived.

A rule is proposed after three moves one way, none the other, and only when three quarters of the newsletter's stories are on that shelf already, so a rule finishes a pattern instead of overruling Jev on a mixed digest.

Checked against my real archive, there was nothing to count. In nearly three weeks of reading in Nutgraf I have never moved a story or an issue to the other shelf, and I have never pulled a promo back. The page shows its empty state. The thresholds stayed: nothing argues for moving them, and three quarters is where Jev's own split divides the 25 newsletters it puts nearly all on one shelf from the 4 mixed ones.

## Why

The issue imagined an agent that rewrites Claude's sorting instructions from my corrections. Since then Jev sorts every story and I froze its question. What is left that is mine to set is a newsletter's "always" shelf, so the tune-up proposes that.

## Where we are now

Milestone 9 is closed; the API and the page are on prod. The page has been checked in mock mode, not yet signed in on a device. Each open reads every story stored, about 10 MB today and growing, so bounding that read is in the backlog (#163).

- No "never a promo" setting, since I have pulled no promo back.
