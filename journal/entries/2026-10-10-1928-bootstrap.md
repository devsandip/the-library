# Bootstrap: from a brief to a sorted inbox

2026-10-10 19:28. First entry. It covers the project from the brief on 2026-10-09 to the morning of 2026-10-10; the next two entries cover the rest of 2026-10-10.

## What happened

I subscribe to about 25 newsletters across two Gmail labels and read almost none of them. On the morning of 2026-10-09 I wrote the brief (`docs/prd.md`): one private reader that learns my taste from Relevant and Irrelevant ratings and puts the best on top, on the iPhone and the Mac. Success means I open it most days and the top 5 are things I would rate Relevant at least 3 times in 5 after two weeks. The name is Harold Finch's headquarters in *Person of Interest*, with light touches: Relevant and Irrelevant, the Machine's yellow box on the top items, "No new numbers." when there is nothing to read.

Claude Code turned the brief into a spec and a build plan, then re-planned within the hour. `sandip.dev` is Amplify Hosting, not a CloudFront distribution we can extend, so the app got its own distribution behind one Amplify rewrite rule for `/thelibrary`, proven by a spike before anything else was built.

Phase 1 went up the same morning. Workspace, hosting, the core library, storage and the ingest Lambda, the spike passing every hosting check, the API behind Cognito, then the reader UI. By 11:37 the reader was live at `sandip.dev/thelibrary`. The afternoon went to Gmail access: public about, privacy and terms pages for Google's consent screen, then the first backfill. It failed 307 of 549 messages on Gmail rate limits and DynamoDB write conflicts. With pacing and retries it stored 394 newsletters and filtered 155 with no errors, and the two-hourly schedule and the staleness alarm went on.

The backlog moved to GitHub issues. Every TLDR edition came from one address, so they were split by name. The regex keep list from the brief matched the wrong newsletters, so a Must Read star replaced it; 11 of 32 newsletters got the star.

Phase 2 started early with Claude enrichment: a one-line summary, News or Learning, one topic and a promo flag per issue, plus a Titan embedding for ranking. Haiku 5.5 is not available on this AWS account, so it runs on Haiku 4.5. Asked yes or no, Haiku called 158 of 396 issues promos, mostly ones that only carry a sponsor slot. A three-way question (none, ad-supported, promo) flagged 42. All 398 issues were sorted, 284 News and 112 Learning, for about $1.61.

On the morning of 2026-10-10 the sender filter, topic chips, the Summary page with ranking at read time and a cleaner look were built and deployed to dev. Prod waited, because the AWS sign-in had expired. I filed #13 to #29: swipe to rate, the story index and the features built on it, and two video ideas.

## Where things stood that morning

- Live on prod: ingest every two hours, the reader, enrichment, the News and Learning tabs, Must Read.
- On dev only: the new reading features and the Summary page.
- About 400 issues stored, no ratings yet.

## What I believed

- Thumbs plus a few interests are enough signal to rank well within two weeks.
- Haiku is good enough to sort and summarize at about half a cent an issue.
- The *Person of Interest* touches stay a garnish, not a costume.

## Open questions

- Will ranking hit the 3-in-5 aim once ratings exist (#5)?
- When will the account get Haiku 5.5?

## Decisions

- Own CloudFront distribution behind one Amplify rewrite rule, proven by a spike first: `sandip.dev` is Amplify Hosting.
- Backlog in GitHub issues, not the build plan.
- A Must Read star instead of the keep list: the keep list's patterns matched the wrong newsletters.
- Enrichment on Haiku 4.5 until 5.5 is available: same price, and 5.5 is not on the account.
- Promo as a three-way question: yes or no flagged 158 of 396 issues, three-way flagged 42.

## Direction changes

- Hosting: was planned as an extension of `sandip.dev`'s CloudFront distribution, now the app's own distribution behind an Amplify rewrite rule.
- Priors: was a keep list of newsletters, now a Must Read star I set by hand.
- Phase 2: was "after a week of ratings", now enrichment first, ranking once ratings exist.
