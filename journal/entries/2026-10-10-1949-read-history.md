# Read history, and four more ideas

2026-10-10 19:49. Previous: [2026-10-10-1930-stories-and-the-day-in-brief.md](2026-10-10-1930-stories-and-the-day-in-brief.md)

## What happened

In the evening I added four ideas to the backlog: tap a story for its summary and double tap to open it (#41), read history (#42), Save for later (#43), and Now reading, one click back to the last article I was reading (#44). I asked for read history first, so it got a new `priority` label and was built straight away (PR #45).

History is its own page. It shows the issues I opened on a day, latest first, with the time I opened each. Earlier and later jump to the nearest days with anything read, and a date field goes to any day. On the Mac it opens issues in the reading pane.

Nothing new is stored for it. Every open was already logged, and each one is now also indexed by time, so a day is one query. The 18 opens from before were indexed once, so my history starts on 2026-10-09, the day the reader went live: 7 issues that day, 9 on 2026-10-10.

## Where we are now

The reader keeps a record of what I read, a day at a time. Save for later and Now reading are next in line.

## Decisions

- Read history before the other three ideas: I asked for it first. It is labelled `priority`.
- Read means opened, the same moment the app marks an issue read.
- One row per issue per day, at its latest open.
- Index the open events that already exist instead of keeping a separate history.
