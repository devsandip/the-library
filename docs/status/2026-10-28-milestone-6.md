**What milestone 6 shipped:**

- [PR 130](https://github.com/devsandip/the-library/pull/130) (#127): the source check runs in the jobs Lambda. `POST` answers 202 at once; `GET` says it is running until the check is kept, or why the latest run kept nothing; a run not finished after 3 minutes counts as stopped. The phone and the Mac poll every 2 seconds, on whichever device opens the story.
- [PR 131](https://github.com/devsandip/the-library/pull/131) (#128): the hunt. One Haiku call per page (`find_original`) on the page's text with links numbered; depth first, no page twice, five pages or 70 seconds; the check, unchanged, reads the original or the story's own page; the path and whether the original was reached are kept with the check.
- [PR 132](https://github.com/devsandip/the-library/pull/132) (#129): the Source tab lists the pages while it runs and, once kept, what the story was checked against and the way there, on the phone and the Mac.
