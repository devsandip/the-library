1 Three directions for The Library

Each column is one direction: how it answers the brief's seven open questions, its look, three phone screens (375 px) and one Mac screen. Same content in all three, a Monday morning on 12 October, so you compare the design and not the data. The ideas come apart, so you can mix them, for example the type of 1a with the command bar of 1b.

Same in all three: the newsletter always sits on its own light page with a margin around it, never recoloured. Swipe right for Relevant, left for Irrelevant and archive, with Undo, starting away from the screen edge. Every AI action shows its time and price before it runs, and results are kept per story. Every view is a URL. 44 px targets, full keyboard on the Mac, reduced motion respected. Fonts are self-hosted in the build; this preview loads them from Google Fonts.
